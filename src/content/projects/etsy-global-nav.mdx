I created a north star vision to guide a new track of work—reimagining Etsy’s navigation.

**My role:** Product Design

<Spacer />

## My approach

I used systems thinking to consider not just the navigation UI, but how navigation connected to landings, page-specific treatments, as well as the needs expectations for different types of users users. Working closely with my PM, we created an opportunity map to inform design explorations and prototypes and define engineering priorities.

That foundation paid off quickly—within a month, we drove $8.1M in revenue with new, conversion-driving features. By the end of H2 2023, our experiments drove **$22.2M** in revenue.
