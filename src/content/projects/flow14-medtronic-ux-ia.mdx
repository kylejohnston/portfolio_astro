In 2019, I worked with Medtronic and an external agency to deliver a user experience audit of Medtronic.com. The audit included 19 user interviews, an analysis of 111 pages, and recommendations for improving information architecture, UX, and navigation as well as page structure and content, SEO, and accessibility.

**My role:** UX, IA, User Research

<Spacer />

## My deliverables
I delivered my findings and recommendations to Medtronic stakeholders, including the Vice President of Marketing.

<Spacer />

## Audit findings
A key deliverable of my audit was a presentation that organized recommendations into actionable improvements. I worked with internal stakeholders, including marketing and Web IT to understand roles and responsibilities and align the opportunities to their internal structure.
