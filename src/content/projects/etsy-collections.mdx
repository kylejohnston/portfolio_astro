## Experiment spotlight

### Collections Sash

I designed a novel feature to help buyers more easily shop collections. This resulted in a 1% lift in conversion, driving $650k in revenue.

Prompted by the question – how can we make it easier for customers to seamlessly shop from collections? – I hypothesized that when people navigate to a listing page from a collection recommendation, allowing them to browse other listings from that collection would increase the likelihood of finding relevant items and lead to increases in conversion and revenue.
