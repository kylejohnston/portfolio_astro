As a 120-year-old brand, VMC Group is a recognized world leader in the design and manufacture of vibration isolation, seismic control and shock protection products.

They wanted to redesign their website to better reflect the growth and maturity of the company and provide a more well-rounded and engaging experience for prospective and existing customers.

**My role:** IA, UX, Visual Design  
**InQuest Marketing:** Development and copywriting
