**My role:** product design, prototypes

## The design challenge

We wanted to personalize Etsy’s homepage using a new LLM-powered dataset, but we knew the existing homepage design wouldn't scale to support the new content. Each module operated independently with different layouts and content sources. And people noticed; in research, we consistently heard the page was “cluttered” and “overwhelming”.

## My solution
I redesigned Etsy’s homepage by creating a scalable system that flexes to support any type of content, all personalized for millions of daily visitors.

### Improvements through iteration

After the launch of the V1 redesign, we continued to iterate through experimentation: 

- Refining design details, copy, and personalization resulted in an additional $3.1M GMS win
- We added 50,000 content groups and extended the new experience to signed out visitors, proving the design system could support a huge variety of users and content
