I led the redesign of favorites on iOS which drove increases to conversion, ad revenue, and page performance.

**My role:** product design & strategy

This redesign accomplished several goals:
- Improved the buyer experience by rethinking wayfinding and surfacing relevant content
- Simplified maintenance and debugging for engineers by eliminating complex UI states
- Made the space more extensible for future product and design improvements

## User insights
Through user research and experimentation, we found the tabbed UI of the control experience limited exploration. Internally, we called this 'tab blindness' — people simply didn't see the feature and left the screen without engaging.

## Design decisions
- I removed tabs in favor of surfacing content on the landing screen, where people expected to find content.
- The four most recent favorites, rendered as larger images, became the centerpiece of the screen.
- This approach aided feature discovery by surfacing other types of favorites and provided space for future product and design improvements. 
- Removing tabs also eliminated complex UI states, simplifying maintenance and debugging for engineers.
