## User insights
Through user research and experimentation, we found the tabbed UI of the control experience limited exploration. Internally, we called this 'tab blindless' — people simply didn't see the feature.

## Design decisions
- I removed tabs in favor of surfacing content on the screen where people expected to find content.
- The four most recent favorites, rendered as a larger images, became the centerpiece of the screen.
- This design approach aided feature discovery by surfacing more types of favorites and provided space for future product and design improvements. 
- Removing tabs also simplified maintenance and debugging for engineers by eliminating complex UI states.
